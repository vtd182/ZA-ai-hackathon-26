# Product fidelity

Make each screen useful enough to review as a product decision, not only attractive enough to present.

## Browse and selection

- Show at least two plausible options when the user is comparing or choosing.
- Give each option decision-making content appropriate to the domain: name, status, price, timing, availability or consequence.
- Make the selected state, unavailable state or recommendation visually legible when relevant.
- Keep the primary task dominant while exposing enough surrounding product context to understand what happens next.

## Recognizable subject

- Depict the actual product, place, person, document or service state whenever it is central to the screen.
- Prefer a real image, ZDS asset, icon, vector or deliberately authored illustration.
- Do not use an empty ellipse, generic rounded rectangle or abstract blob as a stand-in for a subject the user must recognize.
- Decorative geometry may support composition but cannot be the only product representation.

## Journey continuity

- Carry the chosen item, amount, person, time or state into downstream screens.
- Make success and recovery screens explain what happened, what did not happen and the next safe action.
- Keep sample data internally consistent across the complete prototype.

## Review test

At screenshot time ask: could a PM identify a meaningful product assumption, compare alternatives and give precise feedback from this screen? If not, add the missing content or state before polishing decoration.
