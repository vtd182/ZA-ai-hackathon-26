The context switch is killing your momentum.

Designers are in Figma. PMs are in Jira. Developers are in Git. Product specs get lost in chat threads, and no one knows what the source of truth is when the sprint starts.

We built the Kickoff Agent to sit in the middle. It reads your raw ideas, generates structured specs, and syncs them across your ecosystem—all from a local-first desktop app.

Workflow automation, not just a chatbot.

Verified Change Loop

Scope changes trigger impact analysis, before/after previews, and read-back verified syncs. We don't just generate text; we verify the impact.

Multi-agent Canvas

Terminal agents and UI agents collaborating on the same visual tldraw canvas in real-time.

Figma MCP

Direct integration with Figma through our specialized MCP tool. Read designs and map them to product specs instantly.

Local-first Security

SQLite outbox. All external writes require payload-hash approval. Your IP stays on your machine.
